# JourneyX — Brand Source of Truth

## Brand essence
Turn fragmented interactions into one understandable journey.

## Brand promise
JourneyX makes the relationship between interactions visible, connected, and actionable.

## Personality
Intelligent, precise, analytical, trustworthy, premium, technical, modern, calm, enterprise-ready, distinctive.

## Core visual concept
**Convergent Thread** — multiple fragmented signals resolve into one continuous route. The symbol avoids literal J/X construction and avoids generic nodes, infinity loops, AI brains, pins, arrows, and neon effects.

## Logo system
Primary horizontal, stacked, symbol-only, dark-background, monochrome and favicon variants are supplied as SVG.
- Clear space: at least 0.5× the symbol width on every side.
- Minimum digital size: 24 px symbol; 120 px horizontal lockup.
- Favicon: use the supplied 1:1 symbol, no wordmark.
- Never stretch, skew, outline, rotate, add shadows, or introduce gradients.
- Accent teal is optional; the mark must retain recognition in one color.

## Iconography
Use a 1.75 px optical stroke at 16 px scale; round joins/caps; restrained geometry; outline-first; filled forms reserved for states and emphasis. Recommended family: Lucide, customized to match the stroke rules.

## Journey visual language
- Customer: circular identity anchor
- Event: compact rectangular pulse
- Channel: consistent container badge
- Journey: continuous route line
- Transition: short directional bend, never a decorative arrow
- Friction: split/roughened segment
- Escalation: vertical lift / doubled route
- Drop-off: fading or interrupted route
- Resolution: route converges into a filled node
- Churn signal: route exits the main path
- Confidence: line opacity + pattern density, never color alone

## Data visualization
NORMAL = solid route
FRICTION = dashed / broken route
WARNING = amber + triangle marker
CRITICAL = red + double marker
RESOLVED = filled teal endpoint
UNKNOWN = neutral gray + dotted treatment
High confidence = solid line
Medium = mixed dash
Low = dotted
All states remain distinguishable in grayscale.

## Motion
Fragmentation: 80–120 ms separation.
Connection: 120–180 ms convergence.
Flow: 500–700 ms route traversal.
Resolution: 180–240 ms settle.
Keep motion subtle, fast and purposeful. No flashy AI pulses.

## Voice
Concise, intelligent, confident. Prefer concrete verbs: connect, trace, resolve, explain, detect, understand.
Avoid: “unlock the power of AI,” “revolutionize your business,” “next-generation AI platform.”

## Tagline
**See the journey behind every interaction.**

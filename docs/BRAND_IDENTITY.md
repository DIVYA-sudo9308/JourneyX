## 1. Brand Positioning

### Brand Essence

**"Clarity from fragmentation."**

JourneyX exists at the intersection of identity intelligence and journey continuity. It transforms the chaos of disconnected customer interactions into coherent, actionable narratives.

### Brand Promise

Every customer interaction has a story. JourneyX reveals the complete narrative — no gaps, no guesses, no blind spots.

### Brand Personality

JourneyX is the **forensic analyst** of customer experience — methodical, sharp, quietly confident. It doesn't shout about AI or make grandiose claims. It shows you what happened, connects the dots you couldn't see, and lets the data speak with precision.

| Trait | Expression |
|---|---|
| Intelligent | Reveals patterns others miss |
| Precise | Exact identity resolution, not probabilistic hand-waving |
| Measured | Confidence scores, not absolutes |
| Authoritative | Enterprise-grade, not experimental |
| Composed | Calm under data complexity |

### Brand Character

If JourneyX were a professional, it would be a senior intelligence analyst — someone who takes fragmented signals from multiple sources, cross-references them silently, and delivers a single coherent briefing. No drama. No noise. Just the picture that was always there, finally made visible.

### Brand Attributes

| Attribute | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Connection | Links identities and events across channels |
| Continuity | Preserves the thread through every touchpoint |
| Resolution | Both "identity resolution" and "problem resolution" |
| Intelligence | Pattern recognition, not just data aggregation |
| Clarity | Makes the complex legible |
| Movement | Journeys are inherently directional |
| Trust | Confidence-scored, transparent, verifiable |

### Emotional Positioning

**"Finally, I can see the whole picture."**

The emotional outcome is relief and confidence — the feeling of fog lifting. Decision-makers stop guessing. Support teams stop asking customers to repeat themselves. Product teams stop building on incomplete data.

### Functional Positioning

Cross-channel identity resolution and event stitching that transforms siloed customer interactions into unified, analyzable journeys with actionable intelligence.

### Differentiation

| Competitor Pattern | JourneyX Difference |
|---|---|
| CDPs that ingest but don't stitch | Identity-first resolution engine |
| Analytics tools that track single channels | Cross-channel journey stitching |
| AI platforms that predict without explaining | Confidence-scored, transparent resolution |
| Dashboards that visualize but don't diagnose | Journey intelligence with friction/escalation detection |

## 2. Brand Concept Development

### Concept A — Signal Convergence

**Idea:** Multiple distinct signal lines converging into a single coherent path.

**Visual metaphor:** Think of radio signals from different stations that, when properly tuned, reveal the same broadcast. Separate lines of varying character (dashed, dotted, solid) converging into one continuous, confident line.

**Why it fits:** Directly mirrors the product's core function — taking fragmented channel data and resolving it into one journey.

**Strengths:** Immediately communicable. Strong logo potential. Works at every scale. The convergence moment is visually satisfying and emotionally resonant.

**Weaknesses:** Could feel overly linear if not handled with sophistication. Risk of looking like a generic "merge" diagram.

**Scalability:** Excellent. The convergence metaphor extends to icons, loading states, data visualization, transitions, and marketing materials.

**Logo potential:** Very strong — a minimal convergence mark is distinctive and scalable.

**UI potential:** Strong — convergence animations for loading states, journey visualization inherits the language naturally.

---

### Concept B — The Lattice

**Idea:** An interconnected lattice structure where nodes represent identities and edges represent resolved connections.

**Visual metaphor:** A crystalline lattice — structured, geometric, with implied depth. Not a random network graph, but an ordered structure that emerges from chaos.

**Why it fits:** Identity resolution is fundamentally about finding structure in apparent disorder. A lattice communicates both connection and precision.

**Strengths:** Feels technical and premium. Strong geometric foundation for a design system. Communicates complexity without chaos.

**Weaknesses:** Risk of feeling cold or abstract. Could be confused with blockchain/crypto visual language. Harder to make emotionally resonant.

**Scalability:** Good for patterns and textures. Less strong as a standalone symbol.

**Logo potential:** Medium — lattice structures are harder to reduce to a simple mark without losing meaning.

**UI potential:** Good for backgrounds and decorative elements. Less useful as a primary interaction metaphor.

---

### Concept C — The Thread

**Idea:** A single continuous thread that passes through different materials/contexts, maintaining its identity throughout.

**Visual metaphor:** A thread woven through fabric — visible on one side, hidden on the other, but always continuous. The thread is the customer; the fabrics are channels.

**Why it fits:** "Journey" is inherently about continuity. The thread metaphor communicates persistence of identity across contexts — you lose sight of it, but it's always there.

**Strengths:** Emotionally resonant. Communicates continuity powerfully. Distinctive in the analytics/SaaS space. Has a tactile, human quality that balances the technical nature of the product.

**Weaknesses:** Could feel too soft for an enterprise product. "Thread" might read as messaging/chat. Harder to make feel precise and technical.

**Scalability:** Moderate. Works well for brand storytelling but harder to systematize across an entire design system.

**Logo potential:** Medium — thread-based marks risk looking like textile or fashion brands.

**UI potential:** Limited as a primary visual system. Better as a conceptual underpinning.

---

### Concept D — Resolve (Fragmentation → Clarity)

**Idea:** The visual transition from fragmented/scattered elements to resolved/ordered ones. Not the destination — the *process* of resolution.

**Visual metaphor:** Imagine looking through frosted glass that progressively clears. Or scattered particles that find their arrangement. The brand captures this *moment of resolution* — the instant where noise becomes signal.

**Why it fits:** The product's name contains "journey" but its actual magic is *resolution*. This concept centers the transformative moment.

**Strengths:** Dynamic and distinctive. Captures the product's value proposition in a single visual gesture. Creates strong before/after storytelling potential. Feels intelligent and precise.

**Weaknesses:** The "fragmented" state could look like an error or broken design if not handled carefully. Harder to express in static contexts.

**Scalability:** Strong — the fragmented-to-resolved spectrum applies to loading states, confidence visualization, data quality indicators, and the entire journey visualization system.

**Logo potential:** Strong — a mark that is partially fragmented and partially resolved is visually arresting and unique.

**UI potential:** Excellent — every data state (unknown, partial, confident, verified) maps naturally to the fragmentation-resolution spectrum.

---

### Concept E — Waypoints

**Idea:** Sequential markers along a path, each carrying signal about what happened at that point.

**Visual metaphor:** Not GPS pins — more like seismograph readings along a timeline. Each waypoint carries information density (amplitude, color, shape) that communicates what happened there.

**Why it fits:** Journeys are sequences of events. Waypoints honor the temporal, sequential nature of customer experience.

**Strengths:** Maps directly to the journey timeline UI. Each waypoint can carry rich information.

**Weaknesses:** Could feel like a generic timeline tool. Less conceptually distinctive. The metaphor is somewhat literal.

**Scalability:** Good for the product UI. Less distinctive as a brand identity.

**Logo potential:** Weak — hard to differentiate from navigation/mapping brands.

**UI potential:** Strong but generic — most analytics products use some form of waypoint/timeline visualization.

---

### Selected Direction: Concept D — Resolve

**Rationale:**

Concept D (Resolve) is the strongest brand direction for JourneyX because:

1. **It captures the product's core value** — not just showing journeys, but the *transformation* from fragmented data to clear intelligence.

2. **It's visually distinctive** — no other analytics platform has claimed the fragmentation-to-resolution visual space.

3. **It scales across every touchpoint** — from logo to loading states to data confidence indicators to the journey visualization system itself.

4. **It embeds naturally into the UI** — data states (unknown → partial → confident → verified) map directly to the visual spectrum, making the brand and the product inseparable.

5. **It communicates intelligence** — the resolution process implies analytical power without needing to say "AI" or "smart."

6. **It has emotional resonance** — the moment of clarity is universally satisfying.

The execution must be precise and subtle. This is not a "broken glass reassembling" effect. It's the quiet transition from ambiguity to certainty — like an out-of-focus image finding perfect sharpness.

## 3. Logo System

### Design Philosophy

The JourneyX logo captures the **moment of resolution** — the transition from fragmented signals to coherent identity. The mark uses geometric precision to communicate analytical intelligence while the fragmentation element adds visual tension and distinctiveness.

### Primary Symbol — "The Resolve Mark"

**Construction:**

The symbol is built from a geometric base: the letter **X** constructed from two intersecting paths. The left/upper portions of the X are rendered as **fragmented segments** — discrete, separated marks with gaps between them. As the paths move toward the center intersection, the segments become **continuous and solid**. The right/lower portions of the X are complete, confident strokes.

**Geometry:**

- Based on a square grid with 1:1 proportions
- Stroke terminals are cut at consistent 45° angles
- The intersection point sits at the exact geometric center
- Fragment gaps follow a mathematical reduction: gaps decrease by the golden ratio as they approach the center
- Minimum 3 fragments, maximum 5 fragments per arm to maintain legibility

**Visual effect:** When viewed, the eye naturally travels from fragmented to resolved — the symbol *performs* what the product does.

### Wordmark

**"JourneyX"**

- Set in the primary brand typeface (see §5 Typography)
- The "X" in the wordmark is optionally replaced by a simplified version of the Resolve Mark at display sizes (≥ 48px)
- At small sizes (< 24px), the wordmark uses a standard typographic "X" for legibility
- Letter-spacing: +2% of em width
- The "J" has no descender modification — standard typographic form
- Weight: Medium (500) for the word "Journey," Semibold (600) for "X"

### Lockups

**Horizontal lockup:**
Symbol → 1.5x symbol-width gap → Wordmark. Vertically centered on the symbol's center of mass.

**Stacked lockup:**
Symbol centered above wordmark. Gap = 0.75x symbol height. Used when horizontal space is constrained (< 200px width available).

### App Icon

- The Resolve Mark centered within a rounded-square container (iOS: superellipse, Android: adaptive circle)
- Container fill: primary brand color (Deep Indigo)
- Mark rendered in white at 60% of container width
- No fragmentation detail below 32px — at small sizes, use the simplified solid X mark

### Favicon

- 16×16 / 32×32: Simplified Resolve Mark — solid X with a single notch on the upper-left arm to hint at fragmentation
- Single color, no gradients
- Must be legible against both light and dark browser chrome

### Color Versions

| Version | Usage |
|---|---|
| Full color (Deep Indigo symbol + dark text wordmark) | Light backgrounds, primary usage |
| Reversed (White symbol + white wordmark) | Dark backgrounds, hero sections |
| Monochrome dark (single dark color) | Print, single-color contexts |
| Monochrome light (single light color) | Dark single-color contexts |

### Clear Space

Minimum clear space around the logo = the height of the "J" in the wordmark (or 25% of symbol height if symbol is used alone) on all sides.

### Minimum Sizes

| Format | Minimum Width |
|---|---|
| Horizontal lockup | 120px digital / 30mm print |
| Symbol only | 24px digital / 6mm print |
| Favicon | 16px |
| Stacked lockup | 80px wide |

### Usage Restrictions

- Never rotate the logo
- Never stretch or distort
- Never apply drop shadows or outer glows
- Never place on busy photographic backgrounds without a solid overlay
- Never recreate the fragmentation effect freehand — always use the defined geometry
- Never animate the fragmentation in the logo mark itself (motion language applies to UI, not the logo)

### Palette Recommendation: Palette A — Deep Indigo

**Rationale:**

1. **Trust and intelligence** — Indigo is the color of deep analysis. It's more distinctive than standard corporate blue, warmer than navy, and avoids the overused tech-teal and startup-purple.

4. **Chart compatibility** — The indigo-to-amber spectrum provides a natural sequential scale for confidence/intensity. Categorical charts can use well-spaced hues that don't clash with the UI chrome.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|

### Size System

| Size | Pixel | Use |
|---|---|---|
| XS | 14px | Inline with caption text, tight UI |
| SM | 16px | Inline with body text, table actions |
| MD | 20px | Navigation items, card actions |

### Filled vs. Outlined

- **Outlined (default):** Used for navigation, actions, and general UI
- **Filled:** Used for active/selected states (e.g., active nav item, selected filter). The fill transition from outline → filled communicates state change without needing color change alone (accessibility).

## 7. Data Visualization Language

### Semantic Visual System

JourneyX uses a consistent visual language across all data representations. This language uses **three simultaneous channels** — color, shape, and pattern — so no meaning depends on color alone.

### State Definitions

| State | Meaning | Color (Light) | Color (Dark) | Shape Modifier | Pattern |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | Healthy interaction, expected flow | `#059669` | `#34D399` | Solid circle/line | Solid stroke |
| Active | Currently in progress | `#2D3A8C` | `#818CF8` | Pulsing dot | Solid stroke |
| Friction | Delay, repetition, negative signal | `#D97706` | `#FBBF24` | Triangle warning marker | Dashed stroke |
| Warning | Potential churn risk, degrading pattern | `#EA580C` | `#FB923C` | Diamond marker | Dotted stroke |
| Critical | Active churn signal, SLA breach, failure | `#DC2626` | `#F87171` | Square/stop marker | Double stroke |
| Resolved | Issue identified and addressed | `#059669` | `#34D399` | Checkmark overlay | Solid stroke |
| Unknown | Insufficient data for classification | `#94A3B8` | `#475569` | Dashed circle (open) | Stippled/faded |

### Confidence Visualization

Confidence is core to JourneyX's identity resolution. Visualize it as a **segmented strength indicator** (4 segments):

| Confidence Level | Segments Filled | Label | Color |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–25% | 1 of 4 | Low | `#DC2626` / Error |
| 26–50% | 2 of 4 | Partial | `#D97706` / Warning |
| 51–80% | 3 of 4 | High | `#2D3A8C` / Primary |
| 81–100% | 4 of 4 | Verified | `#059669` / Success |

Never show raw percentages without the segmented indicator. The visual reinforcement ensures quick scanning in tables and cards.

### Channel Encoding

Each channel has a consistent **icon + color accent** across all visualizations:

| Channel | Accent Color (Light) | Accent Color (Dark) | Icon |
|---|---|---|---|
| Web | `#2563EB` | `#60A5FA` | Browser |
| Mobile App | `#7C3AED` | `#A78BFA` | Smartphone |
| Email | `#0891B2` | `#67E8F9` | Mail |
| Call Center | `#059669` | `#34D399` | Headset |
| In-Store | `#D97706` | `#FBBF24` | Store |
| Chat | `#9333EA` | `#C084FC` | Message |

### Chart Rules

1. **Maximum 6 series** per chart. Beyond 6, use interactive filtering or grouping.
2. **Always include a legend** when using more than 2 series.
3. **Left-align Y-axis labels.** Right-align secondary Y-axis if dual-axis is necessary.
4. **Grid lines:** Use `Border` color at 50% opacity. Horizontal only unless both axes carry quantitative data.
5. **No 3D effects.** No skeuomorphic styling. No gratuitous gradients.
6. **Tooltips:** Dark background (`#1E293B`), white text, rounded corners (6px), 8px padding.
7. **Annotations:** Use the accent color (Amber Signal) for callout lines and markers.
8. **Empty states:** Show the chart skeleton (axes, grid) in `Disabled Text` color with a centered message.

## 8. Journey Visual Language

### Core Principle

The unified journey timeline is JourneyX's hero visualization. It must feel **proprietary and distinctive** — not a generic flowchart, not a standard Gantt chart, not a basic timeline.

### Visual Grammar

#### Nodes (Events)

Each event in a journey is a **node**. Nodes communicate:

| Property | Encoding |
|---|---|
| Channel | Color accent ring (see §7 channel encoding) |
| Event type | Icon inside the node |
| Sentiment/outcome | Fill state (solid = positive, hollow = neutral, marked = negative) |
| Importance | Node size (3 sizes: minor, standard, key moment) |

**Node construction:**
- Base: 32px circle (standard), 24px (minor), 40px (key moment)
- Channel accent: 3px colored ring on the outer edge
- Interior: Icon from the icon system (§6) at 60% of node diameter
- State: Fill follows the semantic state system (§7)

#### Paths (Connections)

Paths connect sequential events in the journey.

| Path Property | Encoding |
|---|---|
| Normal transition | 2px solid line, `Secondary Text` color |
| Cross-channel transition | 2px solid line with a channel-color gradient between the two channel accent colors |
| Time gap (short) | 2px solid line, standard spacing |
| Time gap (long) | 2px dashed line with time label on the gap |
| Friction | 2px line with amber dash pattern and triangle friction marker |
| Escalation | Path curves upward with an escalation icon at the inflection |
| Drop-off | Path fades to transparent with a terminal marker |

#### Timeline

- Horizontal axis represents time, flowing left to right
- Time labels appear below the path at regular intervals
- The timeline is **not linearly scaled** — it uses an intelligent compression that:
  - Shows recent events at higher resolution
  - Compresses long gaps with a visual break indicator (dashed gap + duration label)
  - Maintains readable spacing between events regardless of actual time gaps

#### Identity Indicators

When multiple identities resolve to the same customer, show the **convergence moment**:

- Before resolution: Separate path lines (one per identity fragment)
- Resolution point: Paths converge into a single node with the "Resolve" indicator (matches the brand mark concept)
- After resolution: Single unified path

The convergence visualization directly echoes the logo concept (Concept D — Resolve).

#### State Change Markers

| Marker | Visual |
|---|---|
| Channel switch | Vertical bridge between horizontal channel lanes |
| Sentiment shift | Color transition on the path stroke |
| Escalation | Upward curve in the path with an icon |
| Resolution | Downward curve returning to baseline with check icon |
| Drop-off | Path opacity fades to 0 with a terminal cap |
| Re-engagement | New path beginning with a "return" arrow from the drop-off point |

### Journey Composition

A complete journey visualization has these layers (bottom to top):

1. **Timeline axis** — dates/times along the bottom
2. **Channel lanes** — subtle horizontal bands (one per channel involved)
3. **Paths** — connection lines between events
4. **Nodes** — event markers on the paths
5. **Markers** — friction, escalation, resolution indicators on paths
6. **Annotations** — hover cards, selected event details

### Interactive States

| Interaction | Effect |
|---|---|
| Hover on node | Expand node to 1.5x with detail tooltip |
| Click on node | Open event detail panel, highlight connected path |
| Hover on path | Show transition duration and channel info |
| Click on friction marker | Open friction detail with contributing signals |
| Zoom | Reveal/collapse event detail (semantic zoom) |
| Pan | Scroll through timeline |

## 9. Motion Language

### Principles

| Principle | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Resolve** | Motion moves from scattered/uncertain to ordered/certain | Loading → data appearing and finding position |
| **Flow** | Motion follows the journey's temporal direction (left → right) | New events slide in from the right |
| **Converge** | Multiple elements moving toward a unified state | Identity merge animation |
| **Reveal** | Information appears through a clarity transition, not a sudden pop | Tooltip fading in, panel sliding open |

### Timing

| Duration | Usage |
|---|---|
| 100ms | Micro-interactions: button hover, focus ring |
| 200ms | State changes: toggle, checkbox, tab switch |
| 300ms | Content transitions: panel open/close, card expand |
| 500ms | Meaningful transitions: page navigation, journey load |
| 800ms | Narrative moments: identity resolution animation, first-load reveal |

**Easing:** Use `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (ease-out-expo) for entrances. Use `cubic-bezier(0.33, 1, 0.68, 1)` (ease-out-cubic) for exits and state changes.

### Specific Motions

**Page load / Journey load:**
- Skeleton appears immediately (0ms)
- Data flows in from left to right along the journey timeline (500ms staggered, 50ms between nodes)
- Nodes emerge from a slightly scattered state to their final positions — a subtle 4px drift resolved over 300ms
- This is the "Resolve" motion — the brand concept in action

**Identity resolution moment:**
- Two or more path lines animate from separate positions toward a convergence point
- At convergence, a subtle ripple emanates outward (800ms)
- The resolved single path draws forward
- This is the signature animation of the product

**Data confidence building:**
- When confidence changes (e.g., new data confirms an identity), the confidence segments fill sequentially (200ms each, left to right)
- The color transitions from the current level to the new level

**Alerts and friction:**
- Friction markers appear with a brief lateral shake (2px, 200ms, 2 cycles) — signaling disruption
- Critical alerts use a single pulse of the error color (opacity 0 → 0.15 → 0 on the background, 600ms)

**Empty → populated transitions:**
- When data loads into an empty state, elements don't just appear — they resolve from a fragmented/faded state to solid (the brand concept)
- Opacity 0.3 → 1.0, position drift 4px → 0px, over 300ms

### What NOT to animate

- Logo (the mark is static — its tension is structural, not temporal)
- Data table cells (too many elements, would create visual noise)
- Axis labels and legends (distracting)
- Navigation chrome (should feel instant and stable)
- Any motion that takes longer than 1 second
- Anything that loops indefinitely (except loading spinners, which should be subtle)

## 10. Brand Voice

### Tone

JourneyX speaks like a **senior analyst briefing a decision-maker**: clear, direct, evidence-based, and confident without being arrogant. The tone adapts to context:

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Product marketing | Assured, precise, outcome-focused |
| Dashboard UI | Minimal, factual, scannable |
| Documentation | Clear, instructive, respectful of the reader's time |
| Error states | Honest, specific, actionable |
| Empty states | Helpful, forward-looking, not apologetic |
| Notifications | Brief, prioritized, context-aware |

### Writing Principles

1. **Lead with the insight, not the feature.** Say what the data means, then how JourneyX found it.
2. **Be specific.** "3 cross-channel journeys show friction" > "Issues detected."
3. **Respect the reader's expertise.** Don't explain what a churn signal is to someone who configured churn rules.
4. **Use concrete nouns.** "Journey," "identity," "event," "channel," "signal" — not "thing," "item," "entity."
5. **Avoid:**
   - "Leverage" / "utilize" — say "use"
   - "Unlock" / "supercharge" / "turbocharge" — say what actually happens
   - "AI-powered" / "smart" / "intelligent" as marketing adjectives
   - Exclamation marks in product UI
   - Emoji in product UI (acceptable in marketing/social only)

### UI Copy Patterns

**Headlines:**
```
DO:  "3 journeys showing escalation patterns"
DO:  "Identity resolved across 4 channels"
DON'T: "Exciting insights detected!"
DON'T: "Your smart AI dashboard is ready"
```

**Error messages:**
```
DO:  "Couldn't load journey data. The events API returned a timeout. Retry or check service status."
DON'T: "Oops! Something went wrong. Please try again later."
DON'T: "Error 500"
```

**Empty states:**
```
DO:  "No journeys match these filters. Try widening the date range or removing a channel filter."
DO:  "Set up your first data source to start stitching journeys."
DON'T: "It's empty in here!"
DON'T: "Nothing to see yet 👀"
```

**Notifications:**
```
DO:  "Identity merge: customer_4821 resolved across web and email (confidence: 94%)"
DON'T: "Great news! We found a match!"
```

**Confirmations:**
```
DO:  "Journey archived. Undo"
DON'T: "Successfully archived! 🎉"
```

### Naming Conventions

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Journey | Trip, path, flow |
| Identity | Profile, person, user (in product context) |
| Event | Action, activity, interaction (use "interaction" only in marketing) |
| Channel | Source, platform, touchpoint |
| Friction | Problem, issue, pain point |
| Resolution | Fix, solution, answer |
| Confidence | Score, certainty, probability |
| Stitch | Merge, combine, join (use "resolve" for identities, "stitch" for events) |

## 14. Final Brand System — Definitive Recommendation

### BRAND CONCEPT

**Resolve** — The visual and conceptual identity centers on the transformation from fragmented signals to coherent intelligence. Every brand touchpoint communicates this arc: ambiguity becoming clarity, scattered data becoming unified journeys, uncertain identities becoming confident resolutions.

### LOGO DIRECTION

**The Resolve Mark** — A geometric X where the left/upper arms are fragmented (separated segments with decreasing gaps) and the right/lower arms are solid and continuous. The mark performs the brand promise: fragmentation resolving into completeness. Works independently at 24px+ and pairs with the "JourneyX" wordmark in horizontal and stacked lockups.

### COLOR PALETTE

**Deep Indigo** system — Primary `#2D3A8C` with amber accent `#D97706`. Full light/dark mode palette with 15 semantic tokens. Chart palette of 6 categorical colors tested for accessibility and data readability. Indigo communicates analytical depth; amber signals attention and wayfinding.

### TYPOGRAPHY

**Geist Sans** (primary) + **Geist Mono** (data). 14-level type scale from 12px captions to 36px display. Geist's geometric-humanist character balances technical precision with approachability. Mono variant ensures clean number alignment in dashboards and tables.

### ICONOGRAPHY

**Lucide Icons** (outlined, 1.5px stroke, 24px grid, round caps) + 11 custom domain-specific icons. Filled variants for active/selected states. Six sizes from 14px to 32px.

### DATA VISUALIZATION STYLE

Seven semantic states (Normal → Active → Friction → Warning → Critical → Resolved → Unknown), each encoded with color + shape + pattern so no meaning depends on color alone. Four-segment confidence indicator. Six categorical chart colors maximum. Channel-specific accent colors consistent across all views.

### JOURNEY VISUAL LANGUAGE

Proprietary journey timeline with node-path-marker composition. Nodes encode channel (accent ring), type (icon), sentiment (fill), and importance (size). Paths encode transition quality, time gaps, friction, escalation, and drop-offs through stroke style variations. Identity resolution visualized as path convergence — directly echoing the brand mark. Semantic zoom reveals detail on interaction.

### MOTION LANGUAGE

Four principles: Resolve (scattered → ordered), Flow (left → right), Converge (many → one), Reveal (hidden → visible). Five duration tiers from 100ms micro-interactions to 800ms narrative moments. Expo ease-out for entrances. Never animate the logo. Never exceed 1 second. Motion communicates state, change, relationship, and flow — not decoration.

### VOICE

Senior analyst briefing a decision-maker. Specific over vague. Insight before feature. No "AI-powered" language, no exclamation marks, no emoji in product UI. Error messages state what happened, then what to do. Empty states guide toward the next action. Terminology is precise and consistent: journey, identity, event, channel, friction, resolution, confidence, stitch.

### TAGLINE

**"Every channel. One journey."**
